The problem

Why hair progress is hard to show.

Hair treatments take months, and most clinics still judge them from photos taken at slightly different angles each time.

Photos change with the angle

A small tilt of the head moves the hairline in a 2D photo. Two visits rarely match, so real change and camera change look the same.

Patients forget where they started

PRP, medical therapy and transplant recovery run over many months. By the follow-up, few patients clearly remember their baseline.

Progress is judged by eye

Without a measurement, the doctor and the patient can read the same photo very differently.

How it works

From scan to comparison in one appointment.

What you get

What you record at each visit.

Every scan becomes part of the patient's 3D record, so each follow-up is measured against the same baseline.

  • Hairline positionMarked on the 3D model, so the same points can be measured again at every follow-up.
  • DistancesBetween landmarks you choose, such as the brow line and the hairline, in millimetres.
  • Visit comparisonThe baseline and later visits overlaid in 3D, viewable from any angle.
  • Skin profileA skin analysis report of the face comes with every scan at no extra cost.
  • Consented recordEach scan is stored with the patient's consent and the date it was taken.

Annotation and measurement are included in the Pro and Premium plans.

Who it's for

Built for hair and scalp practices.

Dermatologists, trichologists, hair transplant surgeons and hair restoration clinics use 3D records to plan treatment, set expectations and show progress across PRP, medical therapy and transplant follow-ups. Clinic chains can keep one scanning standard across every branch.

Questions

What clinics ask first.

Does Edebo replace a trichoscope or a clinical examination?

No. Edebo is a measurement and visualisation tool for the scalp and hairline surface, not a diagnostic device. Clinics use it alongside their existing examination.

What do I need to scan the hairline?

A capable smartphone and a room with even, adequate lighting. There is no rig to buy and no dedicated room to set up.

When can I compare visits?

Any two scans of the same patient can be compared, so you can scan at baseline and at each follow-up you already schedule.

Which plan includes hairline annotation?

Annotation and measurement on the face, hairline and body are included in Pro at ₹4,999 a month and Premium at ₹9,999 a month. Prices exclude taxes.
