The problem

Why body results are hard to prove.

Body treatments work gradually, and 2D photos are easily thrown off by posture, breathing and camera height.

Posture changes the photo

Stance, breathing and camera height all change how a body looks in 2D, even when nothing has changed.

Results build slowly

Fat reduction and skin tightening programmes run over several sessions, and small changes between them are easy to miss.

Booths need a room

Dedicated body scanners need floor space and a fixed setup that many clinics cannot spare.

Questions

What clinics ask first.

Do I need a body scanning booth?

No. Edebo uses a smartphone. You need a room with even, adequate lighting and space to move around the patient.

How is privacy handled for body scans?

Each scan is captured with the patient's consent, encrypted in transit and at rest, stored apart from identifying details, opened only by authorised roles and kept in India.

Can I mark up body scans?

Yes. On Pro and Premium you can place markers, draw lines and measure distances on the body, and the markings stay with the patient's 3D record.

Is Edebo a medical diagnostic device?

No. Edebo is a measurement and visualisation tool, not a diagnostic device.
