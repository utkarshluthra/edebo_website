How it works

From a phone pass to a 3D result, in four steps.

Edebo is software. It turns an ordinary phone camera into a 3D scanner, rebuilds the face or body as a measurable model, and has it ready to show the patient before the consultation ends.

Step 1

Capture depth

Move the phone slowly across the face or body. The camera reads thousands of depth points as it goes.

Output: phone capture
Step 2

Build the cloud

Those points form a dense 3D point cloud, the raw shape of the surface captured in space.

Output: point cloud
Step 3

Reconstruct and measure

The cloud is stitched into a solid model, with volume, symmetry and contour ready to read.

Output: measurable model
Step 4

Compare and demonstrate results in 3D

Overlay scans from different visits, turn the model and show the patient exactly what changed.

Output: before and after, in 3D

Step 4 · Compare and demonstrate

Show patients what changed, in 3D.

Every scan sits on the same 3D grid, so two visits line up exactly. Drag the divider to see how a follow-up reads against the first scan.

  • Volume

    Volume and projection. Change from fillers, contouring or reduction, before and after.

  • Balance

    Symmetry and proportion. Left against right, and key facial ratios.

  • Time

    Change over time. Overlay any two visits to show progress at a glance.

  • Surface

    Contour and surface area. For resurfacing, scarring and skin programmes.

Questions

What clinics ask first.

Which phones does Edebo work on?

Edebo runs on recent smartphones with capable cameras. There is no proprietary camera to buy. A confirmed device list will be published here.

How accurate is a phone scan?

Edebo is built for aesthetic planning, follow-up and patient communication. Validated accuracy figures will be published once testing is complete. Edebo is a measurement and visualisation tool, not a diagnostic device.

Do I need any extra hardware or lighting?

No rig and no dedicated room. You need a capable phone and a room with adequate, even lighting. Strong backlight or very dim rooms reduce scan quality.

How long does a scan take?

A single pass of the phone takes seconds, and the model is ready to view while the patient is still with you.

Can it scan the body as well as the face?

Yes. Edebo supports both face and body capture, so the same tool covers your full range of treatments.

Can we integrate Edebo into our platform or product?

Yes. CRM platforms, filler companies and device companies can offer Edebo to their clinics through the Platform plan, with an API and usage-based pricing.

How is patient data handled?

Scans are sensitive personal data. They are captured with consent, encrypted in storage and in transit, and access is limited by role. Read the Privacy Policy or write to support@edebo.in.
