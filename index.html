Questions

What clinics ask first.

Which phones does Edebo work on?

Edebo runs on recent smartphones with capable cameras. There is no proprietary camera to buy. A confirmed device list will be published here.

How accurate is a phone scan?

Published studies of smartphone 3D face capture report a mean landmark deviation of about 0.8 mm against a Vectra M5 reference, inside the 2 mm limit used for clinical acceptability. Edebo's own validation results will be published on this page. Edebo is a measurement and visualisation tool, not a diagnostic device.

Is the skin analysis report charged separately?

No. Every scan returns its 3D model, a skin profile and a skin analysis report, all at the same scan rate.

Can I mark up a scan?

Yes. On Pro and Premium you can place markers, draw lines and measure distances on the face, hairline and body. Markings stay with the patient's 3D record.

Do I need any extra hardware or lighting?

No rig and no dedicated room. You need a capable phone and a room with adequate, even lighting. Strong backlight or very dim rooms reduce scan quality.

How many devices can use one plan?

Starter and Pro run on one device at a time, and Premium on two. The Platform plan covers up to 10 practitioner profiles with 4 devices each.

Can we integrate Edebo into our platform or product?

Yes. Clinic CRM platforms, aesthetic pharmaceutical companies and aesthetic device manufacturers can offer Edebo to their clinics through the Platform plan, with an API and usage-based pricing.

How is patient data protected?

Each scan is captured with the patient's consent, encrypted in transit and at rest, stored apart from identifying details, opened only by authorised roles, and kept in India. Clinic data is not used to train our models unless you opt in. Read the Privacy Policy or write to support@edebo.in.
