Jawline and chin treatments have grown quickly, driven partly by how faces look on camera. They are also among the most posture-sensitive results in aesthetics. A small change in head tilt can create, or erase, a defined jawline in a photo. 3D imaging makes these results measurable.

## Why posture matters so much

When the chin tilts down, soft tissue under the jaw bunches and the jawline softens. When it tilts up, the neck stretches and the jaw looks sharper. Many "before and after" jawline images owe more to posture than to treatment. We explain this in [why 2D before-and-after photos mislead](/blog/why-2d-before-after-photos-mislead/).

Aligned 3D scans solve this because the comparison is made surface to surface after alignment on stable regions, not between two separately posed photos.

## What to assess

### Chin projection

How far the chin sits forward relative to the lips and nose in profile. A weak chin can make the nose look larger and the neck less defined. This links closely to [rhinoplasty consultations](/blog/rhinoplasty-consultation-3d-imaging/).

### Chin height and width

Vertical length of the lower face and width of the chin, which influence facial balance.

### Jawline definition

The contour from chin to jaw angle, viewed obliquely and in profile.

### Jaw angle

Position and prominence of the angle of the mandible.

### Symmetry

Left-right balance along the jawline. See [facial symmetry analysis](/blog/facial-symmetry-analysis-3d/).

## A 3D workflow

1. **Baseline scan** with head level (Frankfort horizontal plane parallel to the floor), teeth lightly together, lips relaxed.
2. **Consultation**: rotate the model to profile and oblique views. Show the patient their chin projection and jawline from angles they rarely see.
3. **Plan and annotate** target areas on the model.
4. **Treatment**: filler, neuromodulator for masseter, energy-based treatments or surgery, as clinically appropriate.
5. **Review scan** after swelling settles.
6. **Compare** with a colour map and measured change in projection and contour.

## Masseter and lower face slimming

For neuromodulator treatment of the masseter, 3D scans at baseline and after the effect develops can show the change in lower face width and contour. Expression and teeth position must be consistent between scans. See [documenting botulinum toxin results](/blog/documenting-botulinum-toxin-results/).

## Profile proportions worth discussing

Many clinicians use simple profile references when discussing the lower face, such as how the chin sits relative to a vertical line dropped from the lower lip, or how the lips sit relative to a line from nose tip to chin. These are conversation aids, not targets. Faces vary widely by ethnicity, sex and personal preference, and Indian patients in particular are often poorly served by reference ideals drawn from Western populations.

A 3D model makes these conversations more grounded. Instead of comparing the patient to a diagram, you show their own profile from a consistent angle, mark the relevant points, and discuss what a change of a few millimetres would mean for their overall balance. Patients often realise that a modest chin change does more for their profile than the nose or lip change they came in asking about.

## Treatment choice and measurement

Different treatments change the lower face in different ways, and the measurement plan should follow:

| Treatment | Main change | What to measure |
|---|---|---|
| Chin filler | Projection and height | Profile projection, chin length |
| Jawline filler | Definition along the mandible | Contour colour map along the jaw |
| Masseter neuromodulator | Reduced lower-face width | Width at the jaw angle, over weeks |
| Submental fat reduction | Jaw-neck angle | Contour under the jaw, neck profile |
| Surgical genioplasty or implant | Projection, larger changes | Profile and contour over recovery |

Recording which measurement matters for each treatment keeps follow-ups focused, and makes it easier to compare outcomes across patients over time.

## Combining with body and neck treatments

Submental fat reduction and skin tightening affect the jaw-neck angle. A single scan that captures face and upper neck lets clinicians track these alongside jawline treatments. See [body contouring measurement](/blog/body-contouring-measurement-3d/).

## Showing results honestly

Show the profile view side by side, turn on the colour map, and give one clear number, such as change in chin projection. Avoid choosing flattering angles for the "after"; the strength of 3D is that both scans can be viewed at exactly the same angle.
