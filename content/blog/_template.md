## First section heading

Use ## for sections (they build the table of contents) and ### for sub-sections.

Link to at least three other posts with root-relative links, for example
[how accurate smartphone 3D scanning is](/blog/how-accurate-is-smartphone-3d-face-scanning/).
The build fails if a /blog/ link points to a post that does not exist.

## Rules for every Edebo post

- Edebo is a measurement and visualisation tool, not a diagnostic device. Never write "diagnose", "clinically validated" or "most accurate".
- Do not invent accuracy figures. Cite published studies by name, or say "Edebo's own validation results will be published".
- Public pricing shows plan scan limits as multiples (2x Starter, 10x Starter), never credit counts.
- Every patient image needs documented consent.
