The problem

Where 2D before and after photos fall short.

Before and after photos are the main way clinics show results, yet they are hard to repeat and hard to keep safely.

No two photos match

Lighting, distance and head angle change between visits, so the comparison is never like for like.

Photos live on staff phones

Clinical photos end up in personal galleries and chat apps, with no consent record and no audit trail.

Flat images hide shape

Volume, contour and symmetry are three-dimensional. A photo can only hint at them.

Questions

What clinics ask first.

Can Edebo replace our clinical photography setup?

Edebo can serve as the visual record for treatments where shape matters, and it needs no camera rig or dedicated room. You can keep 2D photography alongside it where you prefer.

Where is the data stored?

In India. Scans are encrypted in transit and at rest, stored apart from identifying details and opened only by authorised roles. Clinic data is not used to train models unless you opt in.

Can I share results with patients?

Yes. Reports can be shared with patients through a link.

What does it cost?

Practitioner plans start at ₹1,999 a month, with skin analysis included in every scan. Prices exclude taxes.
