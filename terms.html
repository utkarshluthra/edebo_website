01The service

Edebo provides software that uses a smartphone camera to capture 3D models of the face and body, take measurements from them and compare scans over time. These terms cover the website, the Edebo app and any platform or API access ("the service").

Simulations and comparisons are illustrations to support a conversation with the patient. They are not a promise of any treatment outcome.
